    Methods, sources and downloads

    Elevated burden means meeting the fixed 2015–2019 upper-quartile tract rate benchmark. “Below” does not mean no harm. Special-use and otherwise rate-ineligible tracts are retained as unavailable; their counts remain accessible.

    Planned group comparisons describe the typical tract, not the typical resident. Small groups will show individual observations. ACS margins of error and differences between source vintages limit interpretation.

    • Firearm assault injuries: Philadelphia Police Department’s Shooting Victims dataset via OpenDataPhilly, local extraction May 21, 2026.
    • Census ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates.
    • CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 documentation; national tract release.
